# Immigration guidance: Ireland

Published guidance and policy documents that Ireland's immigration authorities issue for (or apply in) deciding visa, residence, citizenship, employment-permit and international protection cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance: 176 files, 120 MB, 2014 to 2026.** `index.tsv` lists each with its title, date, publisher and source address.
  - `isd/` (91): Immigration Service Delivery policy documents, for example on non-EEA family reunification, de facto partners, ministers of religion, the immigrant investor programme, victims of trafficking and the Brexit scheme.
  - `ipat/` (43): the International Protection Appeals Tribunal's chairperson's guidelines and procedures.
  - `dete/` (28): the Department of Enterprise's employment permit policy and guidance, including the ineligible occupations list.
  - `ipo/` (14): the International Protection Office's procedures and guidance.
- **Country-of-origin reports: 931 files, 149 MB, 2007 to 2025**, in `coi/` with their own `coi/index.tsv`. These are the Refugee Documentation Centre's query responses (874) and reports, published through the EU asylum agency's COI portal.
- `not-obtained.tsv` lists what is known to exist but was not fetched. For all jurisdictions see [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md) and the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Some policy documents appear in more than one dated copy, because the publisher reissued them under new file names; all copies are kept as published.

The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
